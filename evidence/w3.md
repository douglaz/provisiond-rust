The implementation extends the existing Store/Transaction/Process ownership boundary.
Offline migration/version checks, admitted dedicated sessions, generation-only startup advance,
first-statement fencing, ordinary restart recovery, atomic claims/guards, tenant advisory
serialization, atomic admission/payment/verified-create settlement, resource exclusions and
component external-call gates use that boundary. Lock loss cancels active create simulator work
and retains bounded shielded records and after-return outcomes. Worker permits reserve capacity
before claim transactions and remain held through execution and recording. Unsupported provider
execution kinds and open recovery incidents refuse their affected path.

The store closes a cancelled pre-COMMIT execute or pending verification transport, while a sent
COMMIT retains its original transport, checkout and component ownership. The whole-checkout
budget stays 250 ms; notice payloads are capped at 16 with a separate sticky COMMIT warning bit.
Raw production caller SQL is structurally absent; independent corrupted-setting fixtures test
the final pre-COMMIT backstop. No real provider, purchase or funds were involved.
